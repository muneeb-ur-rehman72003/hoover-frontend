Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy explains how Hoover Remover Pros Exterior Cleaning Services (“we,” “our,” or “us”) uses cookies and similar technologies on our website. This policy is designed to comply with applicable U.S. privacy laws and widely accepted website compliance standards.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer, smartphone, or other internet-enabled device when you visit a website. Cookies allow a website to recognize your device, store certain information about your preferences, and improve overall functionality and performance.

Cookies do not typically contain personal information such as your name or payment details. However, some cookies may be linked to personal data when combined with information you voluntarily provide through forms.

2. Types of Cookies We Use

Our website uses the following categories of cookies:

a. Essential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for the website to function properly. They enable core features such as:

  • Secure form submissions

  • Navigation between pages

  • Lead request and estimate forms

Without these cookies, the website may not function as intended.

b. Performance & Analytics Cookies

These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our website by collecting anonymous usage data, such as:

  • Pages visited

  • Time spent on the site

  • Traffic sources

  • Device and browser type

This information allows us to improve website performance, content structure, and user experience. Data collected is aggregated and does not identify individual users.

d. Third-Party Cookies

We may allow trusted third-party services (such as analytics or marketing tools) to place cookies on our website. These cookies are governed by the respective third party’s privacy policies.

We do not allow third-party cookies to collect sensitive personal data.

c. Functionality Cookies

Functionality cookies allow the website to remember choices you make, such as:

  • Location preferences

  • Previously submitted form details

  • User interaction settings

These cookies help provide a more personalized and efficient browsing experience.

3. How We Use Cookies

Cookies help us:

  • Operate and maintain our website

  • Improve page load speed and performance

  • Understand user behavior and engagement

  • Optimize lead-generation forms and calls to action

  • Enhance security and prevent fraudulent activity

Cookies are never used to directly sell your personal information.

4. Consent to Cookies

By using our website, you consent to the use of cookies in accordance with this policy. Where required by law, cookie consent banners or preference tools may be displayed to allow you to manage your choices.

Continued use of the website after viewing this policy constitutes acceptance of cookie usage.

5. Managing or Disabling Cookies

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to:

  • View stored cookies

  • Delete existing cookies

  • Block future cookies

  • Receive alerts before cookies are stored

Please note that disabling cookies may limit website functionality and affect your ability to submit forms or access certain features.
